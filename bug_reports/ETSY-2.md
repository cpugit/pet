## Summary:
**The system does not display an error when the "City" field in the personal profile is filled with a numeric value.**

---

## Pre-conditions:					
1. The user is logged into the system.				
2. The page https://www.etsy.com/your/profile is open in the browser.							

---
## Steps to reproduce:

1. Fill in the "City" field with numeric data.				
2. Click the "Save Changes" button.							

---

## Actual result:					
**The message “Your changes have been saved.” is displayed.**		

---			
					
## Expected result:					
**The message “Enter the name of an existing city” is displayed. The “Save Changes” button is inactive.**
