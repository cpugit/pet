## Summary:
**Redirect to another page when uploading a profile image in a format that does not meet the requirements.**

---

## Pre-conditions:					
1. The user is logged into the system.				
2. The page https://www.etsy.com/your/profile  is open in the browser.												

---
## Steps to reproduce:

1. In the "Profile Picture" field, upload an image in a format that does not meet the requirements.				
2. Click the "Save Changes" button.									

---

## Actual result:

---			
					
## Expected result:
